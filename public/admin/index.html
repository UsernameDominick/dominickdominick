                          Overview

                          Individual reports
                          
        
                          
          Sitemap Exclusions
          
                          Pages, articles, and projects currently left out of sitemap.xml (still fully live at their address, and search engines can still find them through links; use "noindex" on the item to ask them not to list it).
                          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          SEO Report
          
                          Pages with no meta description, images missing alt text or with alt text that says nothing (a file name, "image", "image of…", over 125 characters), titles search results will cut off (over 60 characters), and meta descriptions or summaries that are too short or too long, across the whole site.
                          
          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          Accessibility Report
          
                          Scans "Raw HTML" blocks for common keyboard-nav/focus issues, gallery images for missing alt text, and every block's custom text/background color pair for low contrast, site-wide. Heuristic, not exhaustive.
                          
          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          Link Checker
          
                          This also runs automatically once a day in the background, so a link going dead doesn't wait to be noticed here. Links to other sites are checked 25 at a time, so a long list is covered over a few days.
                          
          
          
                          
          
                          Scans every button/project-card link and article link for broken URLs and lists each one.
                          
          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          Orphan Pages
          
                          Published pages/articles/projects nothing on the site links to internally, and that aren't in the header nav either — reachable only by someone who already has the URL.
                          
          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          Content Health
          
                          Pages with no real content, and published articles/projects nobody has touched in over a year.
                          
          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          Content Integrity
          
                          Dangling references that don't break anything visibly at save time — a block pointing at a deleted simulation/scan/song, a project's "Related X" list still naming something that's gone, or a page nested under a deleted parent.
