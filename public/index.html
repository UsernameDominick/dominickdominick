Studio Synth

Make beats, melodies and whole songs right here. There is nothing to download and nothing to sign up for. Play the piano on the screen or with a MIDI keyboard, record into up to ten tracks and thirty-two bars, build drum patterns, shape every sound with dials, and keep your song.

What you can do

  • Record and loop. Up to ten tracks, one of them drums, and from four to thirty-two bars. Use the count-in and metronome, loop record, step record, quantize and swing.
  • Play it your way. An on-screen piano with the notes of a scale lit up above the keys, your computer keyboard, a MIDI keyboard if you have one, and a step sequencer for every block. Any synth track can play chords or an arpeggio, and any drum can roll. Every sound has three EQ bands for cutting or boosting one part of it. Hold a note and move the mouse to bend it, sweep its filter and more (the MIDI button sets what the mouse does).
  • Drums made from scratch. A kit of 22 synthesized drums. Each one has its own volume, tuning, pan and effects, and a row of its own to edit.
  • Shape the sound. Eight oscillator sounds and more than twenty ready-made presets, then dials for the filter, envelope, echo, reverb, drive, vibrato and tremolo. Save your own presets.
  • Edit every note. Set the velocity, random velocity, random timing, chance and release of each note.
  • Keep your songs. Your latest work is kept for when you come back. Save songs by name, download a song file, export the song as a WAV audio file, or copy a link that opens it for someone else. Undo and redo as you go.
  • Start fast. Open one of five starter songs, or drop one of eight drum patterns into a block.

Keyboard shortcuts

The letters Z to M and Q to I play the piano, and [ and ] move it an octave down and up. Space plays and stops. Shift+R records. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste a block, and Delete clears it. Shift+M, Shift+S and Shift+L mute, solo and loop the selected track. Ctrl+S saves the song.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There is nothing to pay for and no ads.
Do I need an account?
No. Your songs stay in your browser, and you can download them as files or send a link to keep or share them.
Why is there no sound at first?
Browsers only allow sound after you click or tap. Press a piano key or PLAY and it starts.
Can I use a MIDI keyboard?
Yes, in browsers that support Web MIDI, such as Chrome and Edge. Pick your keyboard with the MIDI button at the top left.
Does it work on a phone or tablet?
Yes, with touch. A computer gives you the most room, and the page scales up to fill a big screen.
